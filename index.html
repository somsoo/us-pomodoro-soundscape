Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do white, pink, and brown noises differ acoustically?

White noise distributes energy equally across frequencies. Pink noise features decreased high frequencies for a soothing rainfall sound. Brown noise drops higher octaves more steeply, yielding a deep, heavy waterfall rumble ideal for programming immersion.

How does the Screen Wake Lock feature protect workflow?

By engaging the Screen Wake Lock API, your laptop or tablet display remains active throughout long sessions, preventing unexpected lockouts while keeping your streak tiles visible.

Are focus streak tiles preserved across page reloads?

Yes. All completed pomodoros are preserved in browser LocalStorage, keeping your daily streak accurate even if you close or refresh the window.

    🧮 How the timer and log work

    • Countdown: when you press Start, the page sets an end time on your device clock and always shows the time left until then. Pause keeps the time left, Reset starts the interval over, and Skip moves to the next interval without logging anything.
    • Order: a finished focus session is followed by a short break, and every fourth one by a long break. After a break the timer switches back to focus. Nothing starts until you press Start, and the count of four starts over when you reload the page.
    • Focus log: a finished focus session adds its full length (for example 50 minutes if you set 50) to the local date on which it ends. The log keeps the last 400 days in this browser.
    • 28-day grid: one square per day, oldest first and today last. Darker squares mean more focus time: 1–29, 30–59, 60–119 and 120 or more minutes.
    • Noise: each color is a 10-second loop made in your browser with a short crossfade at the loop point, so there is no click when it repeats. Pink noise uses Paul Kellet's well-known filter and brown noise a leaky integrator of white noise.

    ❓ Frequently asked questions

    How does the Pomodoro Technique work?

    Pick one task, set a timer for 25 minutes and work only on that task until it rings. Then take a 5-minute break, and after every four pomodoros take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes. This timer starts with 25, 5 and 15 minutes, and you can change each length.

    What is the difference between white, pink and brown noise?

    White noise has the same power at every frequency, so it sounds bright, like hiss. Pink noise has the same power in every octave, so its power falls about 3 dB per octave and it sounds softer. Brown noise falls about 6 dB per octave and sounds like a deep rumble. All three are made in your browser from random numbers and set to the same average level.

    Does the timer stay accurate in a background tab?

    Yes. The timer counts down to a fixed end time on your device clock instead of counting ticks, so it stays correct when your browser slows down background tabs. The end chime is scheduled on the audio clock when you press Start, so it can play on time while you work in another tab. Time your computer spends asleep counts as time passed.

    How are my focus minutes and streak counted?

    Only focus sessions that run to the end count; breaks, skipped and reset sessions do not. Each finished session adds its full length and is filed under your device's local date when it ends. Your streak is the number of days in a row with at least one finished focus session, up to today, or up to yesterday if you have not finished one yet today.

    Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

    Your focus log and settings are saved only in this browser's local storage and are never sent to us. The sounds are generated on your device and nothing is recorded. Press Clear log twice to delete the log.
